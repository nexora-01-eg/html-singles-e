What you get

Three views your planning cycle actually needs

No dashboard gallery. Each of these answers a decision you have to make, and each one is built on events you already emit.

  • Adoption per release

    Track how quickly each shipped feature reaches your active base, and compare releases side by side without rebuilding the query.

  • Funnels that match reality

    Define a flow from the events your product truly fires. Where a step collapses, the drop-off and the affected segment surface together.

How it works

Three steps, no replatforming

If your product already emits events, you are most of the way there.

  1. Step 01

    Point at your stream

    Send your existing web and mobile events through, or read from the warehouse you already pay for.

  2. Step 02

    Name the events

    Map the events that matter to products, features, and flows. Northwind handles the cohort maths from there.

  3. Step 03

    Read it in planning

    Walk into the planning review with the current numbers and a saved view behind them, not a screenshot from last quarter.
